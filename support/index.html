Contact Bit Me!

For technical support, a missing interaction, or a privacy request, email ttgaryttv@gmail.com.

Never send passwords or secrets. Support does not need your Twitch password, API client secret, Extension shared secret, OBS WebSocket password, or payment-card information.

Report an interaction problem

Include the Twitch channel, your Twitch username, the gift name, approximate time and timezone, and transaction ID if available. Describe whether it was missing, interrupted, delayed, or repeated.

The operator can check available delivery records and investigate a retry. Do not trigger more Bits interactions just to diagnose a failure. For billing or Bits account issues, use Twitch Support.

Private development status

Bit Me! is currently being tested on TTGaryTTV’s channel. A public Bits-enabled release has not launched. Availability checks and successful development tests do not establish Twitch approval or guarantee future release.
