1. About these terms

These draft terms describe Bit Me!, operated by TTGaryTTV. Contact: ttgaryttv@gmail.com. They cover the Bit Me! extension, local streamer companion, and these support pages.

The operator has not yet finalized or published these terms as an effective agreement. The app remains in private testing. These pages do not announce Twitch approval or an available public Bits-enabled release.

2. The service

Bit Me! presents a menu of stream interactions through a Twitch panel. The local companion receives transaction information from Twitch and queues matching visuals and sounds for the streamer’s OBS overlay.

Availability depends on Twitch, the companion, OBS, the streamer’s configuration, and network access. An availability signal reduces the chance of an unavailable interaction; it cannot guarantee uninterrupted playback.

3. Bits interactions

If and when a Bits-enabled release is approved and made available, Twitch’s confirmation interface provides the amount and confirms the interaction. Twitch administers Bits and the associated account and billing information. Bit Me! does not collect card details or operate a separate viewer checkout.

Use is also subject to applicable Twitch Terms of Service and Bits Acceptable Use Policy. Development-mode tests are distinct from a released Bits-enabled experience. Do not assume a public release is active merely because a test panel is visible.

4. Interrupted or missing interactions

Interactions may be delayed, stopped, or fail. The companion keeps transaction and delivery records so the operator can investigate and, where appropriate, retry an interaction. A reported completion is a technical status, not proof that every viewer saw or heard it.

If an interaction does not play as expected, contact ttgaryttv@gmail.com with your Twitch username, the interaction name, approximate time and timezone, and transaction ID if available. Do not send passwords, API secrets, OBS credentials, or financial account details.

Bit Me! does not currently provide automatic Bits reversals or refunds. Questions about Twitch billing or Bits account adjustments should also be directed to Twitch Support. Nothing on this page limits rights or remedies that applicable law provides.

5. Use and content

Follow Twitch’s rules and respect other people’s rights. Do not exploit the extension, fake transaction information, bypass availability checks, or use the service to harass others.

The operator is responsible for reviewing content made available on the channel and for having the rights needed to use its images and sounds. The viewer panel does not provide a free-form media upload or text-request feature. Features must remain within Twitch’s extension policies.

6. Changes and availability

Menus, features, and availability may change. The operator may pause interactions for maintenance, moderation, or technical reasons. Changes affecting the meaning or handling of a previously confirmed interaction should be investigated through the support process above rather than silently treated as fulfilled.
