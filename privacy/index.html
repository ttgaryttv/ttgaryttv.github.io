Privacy Policy

Owner review draft · Updated October 19, 2026

1. Operator and scope2. Information used3. How information is used4. Storage and retention5. Services and information flow6. Your choices and requests7. Updates

1. Operator and scope

Bit Me! is operated by TTGaryTTV. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to ttgaryttv@gmail.com. This draft describes the current private-test architecture: Twitch panel, local streamer companion, OBS connection, and these support pages.

2. Information used

  • Twitch information: channel and extension identifiers; transaction IDs and timestamps; Twitch display names; product SKUs; Bits amounts; and whether a transaction is a development test. Twitch API responses may also contain Twitch user identifiers while processed in memory.
  • Delivery records: the selected gift, queued/playing/failed/completed status, error or recovery reasons, and a temporary overlay identity used to coordinate playback.
  • Streamer configuration: gift names, images, sounds, menu settings, overlay settings, channel information, and connection settings.
  • Support messages: the email address, message, and any information you choose to include when contacting support.

Bit Me! does not request viewer payment-card information. Twitch’s systems handle Bits and their associated billing information.

3. How information is used

Information is used to match Twitch-confirmed interactions to the correct channel and gift, coordinate OBS playback, prevent duplicate delivery attempts, investigate failures, respond to support requests, and protect the service from misuse.

A viewer’s Twitch display name may appear on the stream’s gift alert when the streamer enables that display. It may therefore be seen by other viewers and retained in stream recordings or clips.

4. Storage and retention

The current companion stores gift media, configuration, transaction records, and delivery history on the streamer’s Windows PC. Transaction records are not automatically deleted on a fixed schedule in the current build; they remain until the operator removes them. This helps detect duplicate receipts and investigate delivery problems.

Streamer API credentials and the OBS password stay in memory unless the streamer selects Remember. Remembered secrets are encrypted using Windows account protection. They are excluded from the app’s library backup and distributed update ZIPs. Deleting a saved credential through the companion removes that saved connection file.

The Twitch panel uses Twitch’s configuration service for published menu details. Its optional design-preview mode stores preview menu settings in that browser’s local storage. That preview data remains until cleared or replaced. The companion may keep local server diagnostic logs.

5. Services and information flow

The companion communicates with Twitch to authenticate the streamer’s extension connection, retrieve transaction information, and send short-lived readiness messages. Readiness messages contain channel information, availability, and offered product/price metadata; they do not contain API secrets, OBS passwords, or viewer delivery records.

OBS is checked locally over a password-authenticated connection when configured. Gift media is served locally to the OBS browser source. Channel-menu metadata is sent to Twitch; exporting a menu does not upload the companion’s sound library to Twitch.

Website hosting providers receive technical request information such as IP address and browser information to serve these pages. These pages add no advertising, analytics scripts, newsletter signup, or contact form. Hosting or sign-in services may use their own technical cookies and logs. Email messages are handled by the operator’s email provider.

The current Bit Me! implementation does not include an advertising data feed or a feature for selling viewer information. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address misuse or protect legal rights.

6. Your choices and requests

Contact ttgaryttv@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information held by the operator, or to ask how your information is used. Include enough context to locate the relevant record; do not include secrets or payment details. Identity may need to be verified before acting on a request.

Requests will be handled as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate transaction, security, or legal reasons. Deleting a local record does not remove information held independently by Twitch, email or hosting providers, or copies in stream recordings and clips.

Manage Twitch account data through Twitch’s privacy information and account tools. Do not use the service if you are not eligible to use Twitch under its rules.

7. Updates

This policy will be updated when the release architecture or information handling changes. The page date identifies the latest draft. These draft pages should be reviewed and finalized before they are used for a public release.
